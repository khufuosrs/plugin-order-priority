# Plugin Order Priority

A RuneLite plugin that allows you to fully customize and lock the order of your sidebar icons.

## Features
* **Custom Sorting:** Rearrange your plugin icons into your preferred order.
* **Visibility Toggle:** Hide rarely used icons to reduce sidebar clutter.
* **Persistent Saves:** Your custom layout is saved directly to your RuneLite profile.
* **Clean UI:** Manage your plugins effortlessly with a sleek, native-feeling interface.

![Plugin Order Priority Interface](https://i.imgur.com/R65nhIL.png)

## Usage
1. Click the "Plugin Order Priority" icon on your side panel to open the manager.
2. Select a plugin from the list.
3. Use the arrow controls at the top to move the plugin up or down.
4. Click the eye icon to hide or unhide a plugin from the sidebar.
5. Click **Save & Apply Order** to instantly update your client.
6. Click **Reset to Default** to restore RuneLite's default sorting.
